# 02 — Product guidelines

## The loop test

Every proposal answers one question: **which arrow does this speed up?**

```
creator ──content──▶ viewer ──purchase──▶ seller ──samples+commission──▶ affiliate ──▶ creator
```

If the answer is "none, but users will like it", it goes in the backlog. If the
answer is "it makes two arrows faster", it goes to the front. This test is what
keeps a platform this broad from becoming a pile of features.

## Sequencing, and why it is not negotiable

1. **Attention.** A feed with no supply has nothing to sell.
2. **Commerce.** Only once there is attention worth converting.
3. **Ads.** Only once conversion data exists to price against.

Payouts ship before growth spend, always. A creator whose first ৳500 lands in
bKash tells ten creators; one failed withdrawal ends the relationship
permanently. Growth budget spent ahead of working payouts buys churn.

## Gates, not dates

A phase ends when its gate is met. The gates are in
[../06-roadmap-org-economics.md](../06-roadmap-org-economics.md). A gate is a
number someone can check, never a judgement call:

- P0 → 100 hand-picked creators post three times a week unprompted.
- P1 → D30 retention ≥ 25%, 45+ min/DAU, 20k weekly active creators.
- P2 → RTO under 25%, commission ledger reconciles to the paisa.
- P3 → creator payout success ≥ 99.5%.
- P4 → advertiser 90-day repeat spend rate.

If a gate is missed, the answer is to fix the thing, not to move the gate.

## Defaults carry policy

A default is the decision we make for the ~95% who never open settings, so
defaults are a product position, not a convenience:

- **Bangla**, with an English toggle. Not the reverse.
- **Data Saver on**, 480p on cellular. Prepaid data is rationed by real people.
- **DMs off**, comment filter strict, duet and stitch off. See
  [05](05-trust-safety.md) — protective defaults are why women creators stay.
- **Notifications capped** at ten a day, hard.

## Honesty in the interface

- Published splits stay published. If we take 40% of a gift, the gift sheet
  says 40%.
- A hold is shown with its end date. An invisible hold reads as theft.
- Every enforcement action shows a plain-Bangla reason and offers one appeal.
- Pre-launch copy makes no invented claims — no fabricated user counts, no
  testimonials from people who do not exist.

## What we deliberately do not build

Opaque gift economics. Black-box enforcement. A cross-border-first catalogue,
until the FX and customs path is real. Ads before the feed is dense. Each of
these is a decision recorded in [../01-tiktok-ecosystem-study.md](../01-tiktok-ecosystem-study.md) §7,
not an oversight.
